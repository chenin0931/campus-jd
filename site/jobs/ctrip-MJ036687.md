1.Rotation in different HR functions across all HR’s Three Pillars:

-Master end-to-end HR processes and understand the three-pillar operational model.

-Receive customized development guidance from dual mentors: a Group COE expert and a Regional HR Director.




2.On-the-Ground projects at overseas offices:

-Collaborate with cross-functional HR teams in overseas offices to tackle real-world HR challenges under 
Trip.com
 globalization progress.

-Gain front-line experience solving business-critical HR pain points.




What We Expect From You:

1.Education &amp; Skills:

-Bachelor’s degree or higher (all majors welcome).

-Overseas study or multinational internship experience preferred.

-Fluent in English (written and spoken); additional language skills are a plus.




2.Mindset &amp; Abilities:

-Open-minded, empathetic, and adept at cross-cultural communication.

-Self-driven, responsible, and results-oriented with a problem-solving mindset.

-Thrives in challenges and embraces growth through adversity.




3.Professional Competencies:

-Structured and critical thinking.

-Data analysis proficiency (familiarity with AI tools preferred).

-Strong learning agility and innovation capabilities.







你将会收获：

在COE（专家中心）、SSC（共享服务中心）、事业部HR团队以及海外驻地HR团队轮岗，系统掌握人力资源全流程，理解三支柱运作机制。配备双导师（总部COE专家+区域HR总监）提供定制化发展建议。

我们期望你：

本科及以上学历，不限专业，有海外留学或跨国公司实习经历；能够使用英语进行无障碍沟通；有其他小语种技能者优先考虑。

具备开放的心态，有同理心和跨文化沟通能力，自驱力强，有责任心和推动解决问题的能力，愿意迎难而上，以结果为导向，在挑战中不断成长。
