## 岗位要求

Business Unit Cloud Smart Industries Group (CSIG) is responsible for promoting the company's cloud and industry Internet strategy. CSIG explores the interactions between users and industries to create innovative solutions for smart industries via technological advancements such as cloud, AI, and network security. While driving the digitalization of retail, medical, education, transportation and other industries, CSIG helps companies serve users in smarter ways, building a new ecosystem of intelligent industries that connect users and businesses. What the Role Entails Embark on an exciting journey with Tencent Cloud International as a Sales Intern who is expected to join the sales trainee program based in Dubai after graduation. This role offers you the opportunity to develop expertise in Go-To-Market strategies, build key business relationships, and lay a strong career foundation in the fast-growing public cloud industry. You will receive professional trainings from mentors and fellows and gain hands-on experience in the headquarter in China for the first 6 months or before your graduation, whichever longer. You will grow to play a pivotal role in identifying opportunities and driving success in the MENA cloud market. Proficiency in English and Chinese is essential, and command of Arabic language and living/study experience in the Arabic world will be a plus. Account Management Support: Assist senior sales team members in managing customer accounts, including contract preparation, negotiation, and handling administrative tasks. Relationship Management: Cultivate and maintain strong relationships with stakeholders, offering guidance and support to ensure satisfaction and foster long-term partnerships. Lead Generation: Leverage networking and prospecting skills to identify potential clients, generating leads and uncovering new sales opportunities aligned with Tencent Cloud’s business goals. Sales Strategy and Execution: Collaborate with the Business Contracts Manager
