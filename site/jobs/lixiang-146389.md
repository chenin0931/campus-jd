## 岗位要求

1. Professional background and languages: 2027 fresh graduates; majors in finance, accounting, economics, banking or statistics. English and Russian as working languages.
2. Commercial acumen: Sensitive to numbers and business logic; able to see business problems and opportunities through the numbers in reports, rather than merely completing a calculation.
3. Communication and influence: Articulate and rigorous in logic; able to turn financial conclusions into judgements the business can act on, and willing to hold a professional position in business discussions.
4. Ownership and resilience: Willing to go to the overseas front line, comfortable with multiple tasks and a fast pace, and proactive in finding answers amid uncertainty.
What We Offer
1. A front-row seat in overseas expansion
Join the real operations of a U.S.-listed company's overseas business; work across multiple country entities, multiple currencies and multiple accounting standards in complex operating scenarios and resource allocation; and witness, end to end, a Chinese company going global.
2. A growth path from "doing the numbers" to "running the business"
Your work goes beyond reporting: you take part in decisions on pricing, investment, channels and resources, and test your own judgement against real business results.
3. Continuous mentorship and a broad runway
Start from specific modules and, through mentorship and cross-market exposure, gradually take on fuller business-analysis responsibilities, developing towards regional/country finance BP, product-line/cost BP, FP&A and other directions.
4. Hands-on practice combining AI and finance
Explore how AI can be applied to business analysis, forecasting models and data reconciliation — and be among the first to rebuild the way finance works with AI.
