You will be responsible for:


1. Designing chatbot interaction flows, continuously iterating robot capabilities, and optimizing intelligent customer service experience;

2. Managing daily operation monitoring of AI Chatbot and taking ownership of key service performance metrics;

3. Independently completing Chatbot operation reports based on data analysis, identifying key issues from the perspective of local user behaviors and expectations, and providing reasonable solutions;

4. Working closely with AI product and engineering teams, participating in product and algorithm model optimization processes, and improving the Chatbot’s semantic understanding capabilities;

5. Coordinating with internal business teams to drive intelligent service projects, optimize AI products, and handle Chatbot-related feedback.

 


We are looking for:


1. Native-level or professional proficiency in Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic, MSA preferred), with a strong understanding of local culture; English proficiency as a working language;

2. Excellent logical thinking, data analysis, independent problem-solving, and teamwork capabilities;

3. Knowledge of large language models (LLMs) and AI technologies, familiarity with intelligent conversational systems, and relevant internship experience preferred;

4. Strong communication skills, proactive mindset, high ownership, resilience under pressure, and the ability to adapt and iterate quickly alongside business growth.
