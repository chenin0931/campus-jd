## 岗位要求

Business Unit Cloud Smart Industries Group (CSIG) is responsible for promoting the company's cloud and industry Internet strategy. CSIG explores the interactions between users and industries to create innovative solutions for smart industries via technological advancements such as cloud, AI, and network security. While driving the digitalization of retail, medical, education, transportation and other industries, CSIG helps companies serve users in smarter ways, building a new ecosystem of intelligent industries that connect users and businesses. What the Role Entails Position Overview Tencent Cloud is seeking a motivated Solution Architecture Intern to support enterprise cloud solution design and technical engagement activities in Indonesia. This internship offers hands-on exposure to cloud technologies, solution consulting, and customer-facing projects across multiple industries. You will work closely with experienced Solution Architects, sales teams, and engineering teams to gain practical knowledge of Tencent Cloud products, industry use cases, and solution design methodologies. This role is ideal for students who are passionate about cloud computing and aspire to build a career in solution architecture, technical consulting, or cloud technologies.
