【What We’re Looking For】
Curiosity about platform governance and online communities: A genuine interest in platform governance, online content ecosystems, and broader social issues. You are willing to tackle complex problems without predefined answers and find practical, actionable solutions.
Passion for emerging technologies: A strong interest in AI and other cutting-edge technologies. You understand the capabilities and limitations of LLMs and AI agents, and are eager to turn technological advances into practical product capabilities.
User empathy and systems thinking: The ability to balance thoughtful user empathy with structured, systems-level thinking. You can put yourself in the shoes of users who have been penalized while designing mechanisms and workflows that can operate at a scale of hundreds of millions of users.
Structured communication and cross-functional collaboration: Strong communication and stakeholder management skills. You can explain complex rules clearly and align engineering, algorithms, policy, and operations teams to move projects forward.
Data-driven mindset: You use data and experimentation to validate hypotheses and inform decisions rather than relying solely on intuition.
Deep thinking and a growth mindset: You are committed to continuous learning and improvement, and demonstrate courage and resilience when navigating challenges, uncertainty, and ambiguous situations.
【Nice to Have】
An academic background in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Human-Computer Interaction, Law, Sociology, Communications, or a related field.
Internship or project experience in content platforms, community products, risk control, or compliance.
Strong English reading, writing, and communication skills, with the ability to read and understand regulatory documents and platform policy materials in their original language. This role involves working with global markets.
Cross-cultural living or study experience, or first-hand observations of user behavior in international markets.
Regardless of your academic background or whether you have prior hands-on experience in internet product development, we welcome you if you embody these qualities and are ready to put them into practice.
Join us in taking on one of the hardest yet most worthwhile challenges in technology: building a safer, fairer, and more trustworthy online community.
