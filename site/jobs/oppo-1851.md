## 岗位要求

考虑岗位性质及履职要求，本岗位目前仅面向非中国大陆籍候选人开放.

1.Marketing Strategy: Assist in the development and implementation of marketing strategies to promote products and enhance brand awareness.
2.Campaign Execution: Work closely with cross-functional teams to execute marketing campaigns, including planning, coordination, and monitoring of campaign performance.
3.Market Research: Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and challenges in the smartphone industry.
4.Content Development: Collaborate with the content team to create engaging and relevant marketing materials, including social media content, blog posts, and promotional materials.
5.Analytics and Reporting: Analyze marketing data to evaluate the success of campaigns, track key performance indicators, and provide insights for continuous improvement.
6.Brand Management: Contribute to building and maintaining the brand image through consistent messaging and innovative marketing initiatives.
1. Bachelor’s degree in marketing, Business Administration, or a related field.
2. Proficient in both Chinese (Simplified & Traditional) and English communication is mandatory. 
3. Strong familiarity with the cultural and market environment of Taiwan or Southeast Asia (e.g., Malaysia) is highly preferred.
4. Strong passion for technology and a deep understanding of the smartphone market.
5. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
6. Analytical mindset with the ability to interpret data and make informed decisions.
7. Proactive and self-motivated with a strong desire to learn and grow.
